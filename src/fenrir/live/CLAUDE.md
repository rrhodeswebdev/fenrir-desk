# src/fenrir/live/

Runs armed strategies on live bars: decides per bar, checks limits, journals every decision and stores which strategy is armed on each account. Rolls the trading day (lifting day halts), pauses entries on stale data, emits the orders to send (`Send`), books the add-on's fills, and checks limits against the stricter of its own and NinjaTrader's account figures (halting on a sim/live mode mismatch; a balance at or below 0 is not a figure and is ignored, shown as a `figures` condition while it lasts (a lost link clears it) with one alert per run, and an `end_of_day` roll during it takes no close and halts with "confirm balances", #220; an `end_of_day` roll raises the floor from NinjaTrader's closing balance, but when that floor would breach the app's own figures and not NinjaTrader's own equity (balance plus open P&L), P&L NinjaTrader booked that the app never saw, it keeps the app's figures (its peak raised to its own closing balance), halts with "confirm balances" and flattens any position, never a breach, #219; a later-day restart under that halt is looser by up to the gap, #225). Sim and live start only on NinjaTrader's first account message for the account with a balance above 0, after syncing `armed` so a changed arming never starts (its balance now; phase start and peak from the armed pins; today's open from the journal, else NinjaTrader's balance less any realized loss today, `balance − min(realized_pnl_today, 0)`, so a morning gain gives no extra daily room and is never cut from the balance; a first balance at or below the pins' liquidation level halts with "confirm balances", never a breach), and take no new entries until NinjaTrader's book is reconciled (on start and after a lost link); a mismatch pauses entries until the user resolves it, and a restart replays today's journalled orders and fills. Every start warms up before its first entry (#79, DEC-4e): `Ctx.bars` from the bar store plus today's bars from the LSE feed (which replays the day from its open), today replayed through the strategy with its actions suppressed and compared with the journal. `LiveHub` runs it all for the server: it posts the `Send`s to the NinjaTrader add-on, books the fills routed to it and replays the journal on start (22.4), follows the add-on's event stream (reconcile on connect and while unreconciled, pause on a lost link, account figures, fills) and holds the Resolve and kill-switch actions (22.5) and the Disarm, which flattens a sim or live account before disarming it (#223).

- Pure vs shell: `core.py` is pure `(RunnerState, input) -> (RunnerState, effects)` and never imports strategy code: the shell runs `step(ctx(state))` in a child (DEC-1) and passes the actions to `decide`. `journal.py`, `arming.py` and `service.py` are shell (SQLite I/O, child processes, add-on posts; the hub opens `<private_dir>/fenrir.db` only once it exists, never at app build); `day_bounds` and `decide` are pure (the caller passes `list_armed`, reports, NinjaTrader's `is_sim`, account choices and `now`).
- Warm-up (#79): every start sets `RunnerState.warming` (no entries; exits, marks and limit checks still run, but no step) until `_warm` can replay today: not while the feed's replay of the account's series is unfinished, nor while the bar store misses the last trading day before today (an alert says to run `fenrir data pull`; the hub never pulls). It runs under the hub lock, capped by `WARM_TIMEOUT` (20 s); rows are booked in journal order up to each bar's own "bar" row; a replayed action that differs from the journalled one halts ("warm-up diverged"). Warming is the last of `conditions` (kind "warming", not a halt). `_warm` contains its errors, and `handle_bar` its sync's: none from a bar reaches `run_bars`, which would resubscribe (a full replay). An account with no strategy child (not loaded, or halted and killed) never shows `warming`. While warming, strategy-driven exits do not run. `test_warmup.py` pins the three acceptance criteria.
- Pre-trade decisions must match the backtest's (`fenrir.backtest.attempt._act`); `test_accepts_exactly_what_the_backtest_accepts` pins it.
- `step` sees the backtest's bars: no roll-window bar reaches `step` or `Ctx.bars` (`runs_step`), though `on_bar` still marks and flattens on it; `test_step_sees_the_bars_the_backtest_shows_it_around_a_roll` pins it.
- Fills book the account like the backtest (`attempt._open`/`_close`/`_mark`); `test_live_fills_book_like_the_backtest` pins it. Dry runs never emit a `Send`.
- Pinned inputs (DEC-4d): `core.changed_since_arming` halts an account whose strategy, rules or `instruments.yaml` hash, or `accounts.yaml` `dll_on`, is not what `armed` pinned (or that has no pins), at `start` and on every bar and tick while armed; the hub hashes the very bytes it parses (`read_account`, `read_instruments`) and keeps each account's own `Instruments`. A new input the runner depends on gets pinned the same way, and any per-bar error halts the account, never leaves it unchecked.
- The kill switch posts `/flatten-all` before taking the hub lock, so no tick or bar delays it, and again under the lock before the disarm, so nothing a bar opened while it waited survives; `test_a_position_opened_after_the_kills_first_flatten_does_not_survive_the_kill` pins it. A row the kill could not disarm starts halted and never loads its strategy. `disarm(account)` does the same for one sim or live account with its `/flatten` (#223; `test_a_position_opened_after_the_disarms_first_flatten_does_not_survive_the_disarm`); dry runs never flatten. When the flatten under the lock does not land, or the disarm cannot be written, the row stays armed and the account halted (journalled, so it starts halted and a restart keeps it so), never disarmed with a position that may still be open; a row not running yet is also `held` in memory (as the kill's `killed`), so it shows that reason while it waits and starts halted without loading its strategy, even when sqlite refuses its halt row; the error says when the halt row was not journalled, and when the read of `armed` under the lock failed, that nothing was flattened under it (#227).
- Halts and pauses that must survive a restart are journalled as `"halt"` rows, which `start` restores (as `"peak"` rows restore the drawdown peak and `"day_start"` rows today's open, #211); anything that lifts one in place (e.g. `resolve`) journals a row `start` understands, or the next restart brings it back.
- Never add a CLI command that arms or disarms, and never write the `armed` table outside `arming.py` (SPEC: arming is user-only; the hub disarms only through `arming.delete_armed`, on the user's kill switch, and `arming.disarm`, on the user's Disarm).
- Not here: arm/disarm endpoints (`src/fenrir/server/routes/arming.py`); the add-on client (`src/fenrir/bridge/`); rule math (`src/fenrir/rules/`).
- Tests: `tests/live/` (shared helpers in `tests/live/live_helpers.py`, imported by name).
