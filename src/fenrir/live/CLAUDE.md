# src/fenrir/live/

Runs armed strategies on live bars: decides per bar, checks limits, journals every decision and stores which strategy is armed on each account. Rolls the trading day (lifting day halts), pauses entries on stale data, emits the orders to send (`Send`), books the add-on's fills, and checks limits against the stricter of its own and NinjaTrader's account figures (halting on a sim/live mode mismatch; a balance at or below 0 is not a figure and is ignored, a `figures` pause while it lasts (no new entries; exits and the runner's own Flattens still go out; a lost link sets it too, until NinjaTrader's next account message with a balance above 0, which the reconcile does not lift, #243) with one alert per run, #220 owner decision; an `end_of_day` roll with no close (none yet, a lost link, or during a zero) halts with "confirm balances" and journals no `peak` row, its floor raised from the app's own closing balance and, during a zero, from NinjaTrader's last figures above 0, and an `end_of_day` restart, the same day or later, takes the highest journalled open's peak since arming into its start peak before the liquidation check, so it keeps that floor (a roll during a zero under a stale gap notes NinjaTrader's raised peak as the open's `nt_peak`, which a later-day restart takes as #225 does), #220; an `end_of_day` roll raises the floor from NinjaTrader's closing balance, but when that floor would breach the app's own figures and not NinjaTrader's own equity (balance plus open P&L), P&L NinjaTrader booked that the app never saw, it keeps the app's figures (its peak raised to its own closing balance), halts with "confirm balances" and flattens any position the app holds, and the whole account while NinjaTrader shows open P&L, never a breach, #219, #229; the same for an `intraday` raise on each account message, #222, flattening on that message itself, not only on the next bar; a gap halt replaces another open-ended halt that is no breach (a strategy error, say: #235), never a refused start's (`started` false), and neither does a breach unless the refused start's rules are the armed ones (`_armed_rules`); a refused start runs no strategy, so a position it holds or adopts is flattened by the next bar with an alert, and it journals its `"peak"` rows only under the armed rules, none when its rules file changed or was not pinned (#241), but for its `end_of_day` roll's, journalled under any rules with `refused: true`, which `_missed_close` never reads as a close taken, so a close missed before it still asks to confirm balances (#244); a position booked or added to while a Flatten is out gets its own flatten at once (#234, #237); a manual add on a bracketed position has no known stop (the bracket's stop covers its entry's lots only; `entry_id` tells a later part of that entry) and is flattened at once (#237), and a breach that replaces the halt says it is a loss through the floor the runner checks, not the gap alone; a same-day `intraday` restart starts the peak at least at the replayed balance; under either gap halt the `"peak"` row also journals NinjaTrader's raised peak as `nt_peak`, which a later-day restart, opening at NinjaTrader's balance, takes too, so its floor is never looser than NinjaTrader's, while a same-day restart keeps the app's peak and at least today's open's; such a restart at or below that floor cannot start and alerts why, and a breach after it has that same clause, #225; with that `nt_peak` kept in `RunnerState` from either gap halt (then rising with NinjaTrader's own closes or equity, each rise journalled as a `peak` row's `nt_peak`, review of #246) and restored by any restart (from an open's `nt_peak` too), each account message checks NinjaTrader's own equity against the floor from it, never the app's figures: at or below it the account halts (a gap halt, never a breach; once per run, an alert when the gap halt holds) and flattens on that message, and the status's drawdown room is at most NinjaTrader's own, #239). Sim and live start only on NinjaTrader's first account message for the account with a balance above 0, after syncing `armed` so a changed arming never starts (its balance now; phase start and peak from the armed pins; today's open from the journal, else NinjaTrader's balance less any realized loss today, `balance − min(realized_pnl_today, 0)`, so a morning gain gives no extra daily room and is never cut from the balance; a first balance at or below the pins' liquidation level halts with "confirm balances", never a breach), and take no new entries until NinjaTrader's book is reconciled (on start and after a lost link); a mismatch pauses entries until the user resolves it, and a restart replays today's journalled orders and fills. Every start warms up before its first entry (#79, DEC-4e): `Ctx.bars` from the bar store plus today's bars from the LSE feed (which replays the day from its open), today replayed through the strategy with its actions suppressed and compared with the journal. `LiveHub` runs it all for the server: it posts the `Send`s to the NinjaTrader add-on, books the fills routed to it and replays the journal on start (22.4), follows the add-on's event stream (reconcile on connect and while unreconciled, pause on a lost link, account figures, fills) and holds the Resolve and kill-switch actions (22.5) and the Disarm, which flattens a sim or live account before disarming it (#223), or, on the user's confirmation, disarms one the add-on no longer manages without its flatten (#230).

- Entry points: `core.py` (pure runner: `start`, `on_bar`, `on_tick`, `on_fill`, `on_account`, `send_failed`, `on_disconnect`, `on_reconcile`, `resolve`, `replay`, `client_id`, `in_session`, `runs_step`, `ctx`, `decide`, `warm_up`, `warmed` (#79), `step_failed`, `halt`, `changed_since_arming`, `account_status`, `conditions` (each condition holding entries back, for the strips; #212), `safety_level`, `start_account`; `Setup`, `Start`, `DayStart`, `NtAccount`, `RunnerState`, `Working`, `Log`, `Send`), `journal.py` (`open_db` creates `journal` (indexes `(account, ts)` and `(ts)`, the latter for the all-accounts day read) and `armed` in `<private_dir>/fenrir.db`, in WAL mode so readers never block the hub's writes, and adds the 23.3 pinned columns to an older `armed`; `open_ro` for readers, never creating the file; `write`, `insert` (no commit, for a caller's transaction), `rows` (`skip` leaves kinds out in SQL) and `select` (its parameterised query, for plan tests), `day_bounds`, `JournalRow`, `KINDS`), `arming.py` (`Armed` with its pins (`dll_on`, `rules_hash`, `instruments_hash`, balances; DEC-4d), `list_armed`/`insert_armed` by column name, `delete_armed`, `disarm` (halt row and delete in one transaction); `ArmRequest`, `Refusal`, `decide`: may this file be armed on this account, one `Refusal` per 409 reason; `liquidation_level` for the live confirmation), `service.py` (`LiveHub(settings, *, secret, step_timeout, addon)`: `start`/`stop` from the server lifespan (after `start`, the first sync that finds an armed row starts `run_bars` on `stream_bars`; a sync that finds none stops it, its stream closed, and clears `today` (#251; a re-arm replays today; never from a bar's sync, in the feed task: the next tick)), `tick`, `handle_bar` (keeps today's bars per contract in `today`, dropping a reconnect's repeats; a bar of a series still `replaying` reaches no account), `resubscribed`/`feed_live` (the feed's `on_auth`/`on_live`), `handle_fill`, `handle_stream` (an `account` message starts a waiting sim/live account), `resolve(account)`, `kill()`, `disarm(account, flatten=True)` (#223, #230), `status`; `run_ticks`, `run_stream(hub)`, `run_bars(hub, stream)` (subscribes from the trading day's 18:00 ET open, so each (re)subscription replays today; closes each day's stream before the next subscribes; backs off 1 to 15 min on `FeedStopped`, stops only on a permanent one; `feed_down`/`feed_up` put the reason in `status()` and alert each run (account, `armed_at`) once per outage, at most once per `FEED_ALERT_EVERY` (15 min); `run_bars` passes `on_auth=resubscribed`, which calls `feed_up`, so an authenticated resubscribe clears the error before any bar); pure `parse_reply`), `step_child.py` (`python -P -m fenrir.live.step_child PATH`: one per started account, holds the strategy's state; pure `ctx_json`/`parse_ctx`, and `ctx_lines` for a warm-up's many contexts, each bar encoded once).
- Pure vs shell: `core.py` is pure `(RunnerState, input) -> (RunnerState, effects)` and never imports strategy code: the shell runs `step(ctx(state))` in a child (DEC-1) and passes the actions to `decide`. `journal.py`, `arming.py` and `service.py` are shell (SQLite I/O, child processes, add-on posts; the hub opens `<private_dir>/fenrir.db` only once it exists, never at app build); `day_bounds` and `decide` are pure (the caller passes `list_armed`, reports, NinjaTrader's `is_sim`, account choices and `now`).
- Warm-up (#79): every start sets `RunnerState.warming` (no entries; exits, marks and limit checks still run, but no step) until `_warm` can replay today: not while the feed's replay of the account's series is unfinished, nor while the bar store misses the last trading day before today (an alert says to run `fenrir data pull`; the hub never pulls). It runs under the hub lock, capped by `WARM_TIMEOUT` (20 s); rows are booked in journal order up to each bar's own "bar" row; a replayed action that differs from the journalled one halts ("warm-up diverged"). Warming is the last of `conditions` (kind "warming", not a halt). `_warm` contains its errors, and `handle_bar` its sync's: none from a bar reaches `run_bars`, which would resubscribe (a full replay). An account with no strategy child (not loaded, or halted and killed) never shows `warming`. While warming, strategy-driven exits do not run. `test_warmup.py` pins the three acceptance criteria, and the feed's stop when nothing is armed (#251).
- Pre-trade decisions must match the backtest's (`fenrir.backtest.attempt._act`); `test_accepts_exactly_what_the_backtest_accepts` pins it.
- `step` sees the backtest's bars: no roll-window bar reaches `step` or `Ctx.bars` (`runs_step`), though `on_bar` still marks and flattens on it; `test_step_sees_the_bars_the_backtest_shows_it_around_a_roll` pins it.
- Fills book the account like the backtest (`attempt._open`/`_close`/`_mark`); `test_live_fills_book_like_the_backtest` pins it. Dry runs never emit a `Send`.
- Pinned inputs (DEC-4d): `core.changed_since_arming` halts an account whose strategy, rules or `instruments.yaml` hash, or `accounts.yaml` `dll_on`, is not what `armed` pinned (or that has no pins), at `start` and on every bar and tick while armed; the hub hashes the very bytes it parses (`read_account`, `read_instruments`) and keeps each account's own `Instruments`. A new input the runner depends on gets pinned the same way, and any per-bar error halts the account, never leaves it unchecked.
- The kill switch posts `/flatten-all` before taking the hub lock, so no tick or bar delays it, and again under the lock before the disarm, so nothing a bar opened while it waited survives; `test_a_position_opened_after_the_kills_first_flatten_does_not_survive_the_kill` pins it. A row the kill could not disarm starts halted and never loads its strategy. `disarm(account)` does the same for one sim or live account with its `/flatten` (#223; `test_a_position_opened_after_the_disarms_first_flatten_does_not_survive_the_disarm`); dry runs never flatten. When the flatten under the lock does not land, or the disarm cannot be written, the row stays armed and the account halted (journalled, so it starts halted and a restart keeps it so), never disarmed with a position that may still be open; a row not running yet is also `held` in memory (as the kill's `killed`), so it shows that reason while it waits and starts halted without loading its strategy, even when sqlite refuses its halt row; the error says when the halt row was not journalled, and when the read of `armed` under the lock failed, that nothing was flattened under it (#227). A plain disarm whose flatten under the lock the add-on refused as `unmanaged_account` also answers `unmanaged: true`; only then may `disarm(account, flatten=False)` (the user's confirmed disarm without flatten, #230) skip it: it still posts both flattens and disarms without one only when the one under the lock is refused with exactly that Ack error (never an exception's text), journalling an alert that names the skipped flatten. Any other failure keeps the row as above, and a flatten that lands is a plain disarm, so the flag never skips a managed account's flatten.
- Halts and pauses that must survive a restart are journalled as `"halt"` rows, which `start` restores (as `"peak"` rows restore the drawdown peak, with their `nt_peak` on a later-day restart (#225), and `"day_start"` rows today's open, #211); anything that lifts one in place (e.g. `resolve`) journals a row `start` understands, or the next restart brings it back.
- Never add a CLI command that arms or disarms, and never write the `armed` table outside `arming.py` (SPEC: arming is user-only; the hub disarms only through `arming.delete_armed`, on the user's kill switch, and `arming.disarm`, on the user's Disarm).
- Not here: arm/disarm endpoints (`src/fenrir/server/routes/arming.py`); the add-on client (`src/fenrir/bridge/`); rule math (`src/fenrir/rules/`).
- Tests: `tests/live/` (shared helpers in `tests/live/live_helpers.py`, imported by name).
