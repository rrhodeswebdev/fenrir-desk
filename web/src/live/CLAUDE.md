# web/src/live/

The parts of the Live page (`../routes/live.tsx`) and its full journal (`../routes/live-journal.tsx`) over `GET /api/live/status`, `GET /api/live/journal`, `POST /api/live/kill` and `POST /api/live/resolve` (`docs/contracts.md` "Code strips" and the live API lines beside it; DESIGN.md "Top Strip", "Code Strip", "Kill-Switch Confirmation", "Catalog-Number Headline", "Session Dial", "Segmented Meter", "Sleeve", "Journal Tape", "Motion").

- Entry points: `model.ts` (pure: `LiveStatus`/`AccountStatus`/`Condition`/`KillResult` types; every strip rule reads `conditions` (each condition, even under a halt; #212), never `halt_kind`; `systemStrip` (Feed · Broker · Drift per the contract's code-strip rules: Feed idle with nothing armed, else alarm when LSE is down, caution for any `stale` or `warming` (#79) condition, under a halt too; Broker alarm when NinjaTrader is down, idle while every account is a dry run, so also with none armed; Drift caution when `drift.ok` is false), `stripLabel` (the `aria-label`), `needs` (one per condition: Book, Risk, or "Halt" for a halt no cell names, most severe first; stale and drift pauses are the system strip's, never an account's) and `need` (its first), `mostSevere` (alarm before caution, then live, sim, dry run, then Book, Risk, Halt; ties keep the server's order), `alarmName` (the most severe account in alarm as `<cell> · <account>`; a caution is not named), `nyTime`, `age`, `killCounts` (dry-run positions counted apart as paper), `killText`, `killErrorText`, `KILL_TIMEOUT_MS` (90 s); for #98: `accountStrip` (Risk · Book, each its most severe need: a mismatch under a safety halt lights both), `alarmsOf`, `Pick`/`pickOf`, `focusOf` (a user's pick holds while armed and no account went into alarm since the pick; a new alarm takes focus as `takeover`: the most severe of the alarms that were not there at the pick, which the page records as the new pick; else `mostSevere`, else live, sim, dry run), `canResolve` (a `reconcile` or `drift` condition, under a halt too), `reasons` (the focus field's and sleeve's lines: each condition holding entries back, its reason in its tone, else the unreconciled `halt_reason` plain; then a `figures` condition (#220) in caution, so it never hides the unreconciled pause), `feedDown` (the LSE outage for the strip: reason, and `Retry <NY time>`, `Retrying` or `Restart needed`), `alertTone` (caution also for a `warming` condition, which is no halt), `usd` (cents only when there are any; `signed` for P&L), `price`, `positionText`, `bracketText`, `minis` (rounded to 0.1: `qty × 0.1` drifts in floats), `meters` (drawdown left, daily loss left or "No limit", contracts in minis, target in evaluation; the safety fraction is where the session's `safety` equity level falls on that meter; `alarm`: `full` for loss limits, `over` for contracts, null for the target), `segments` (20 cells: Steel, Ember from the safety fraction on, Red per `alarm`), `meterLabel`, `resolveText` (404 = "not running", never "not armed"; 500 = the pause may already be lifted; no answer in `RESOLVE_TIMEOUT_MS`, 90 s)). `dial.ts` (pure geometry in a 600 viewBox: angle 18:00 → 17:00 New York, radius floor → target (funded: as far above the start as the floor is below); rings (floor, safety, open = `session.start`, target) with signed labels and at-risk flags, labels above their rings pushed 28 units apart; the New York offset read once per call (clocks never change during a session); the trace ends at the stricter equity `start + today_pnl` (the hub's figure), so the dot never sits above it; overnight/live split at 09:30; flat-by spoke at 16:40, the runner's cap, since the status has no `flat_by`; `dialLabel`). `journal.ts` (pure: `JournalRow`, `tradingDay` (New York date six hours on, as `core.sessions.trading_day`), `merge` newest first, `tape` (latest 12, no `bar`, `peak` or `day_start` rows), `journalLine` (`halt` of payload kind `reconcile` → MISMATCH Red; other halts and alerts Ember; `resolve` → RESOLVE and rejections → REJECT in Slate; `day_start` → OPEN in Slate, `open $<balance> · peak $<peak>`, #211), `journalPollMs` (5 s)). `actions.tsx` (shared by `/live` and `/live/journal`: `statusPollMs`, `useLiveStatus` (`fresh` is undefined while the last read failed), `resolveInit` (the Resolve request with its timeout signal), `useKill` and `useResolve` (mutations keyed `['live', 'kill']` and `['live', 'resolve']`, read back with `useMutationState`, so both pages share them and a page change mid-request keeps the button disabled; client timeouts), `LiveStrip` (system strip and Kill all), `KillLine`, `ResolveLine` (stamped with the New York time pressed)). `parts.tsx` (`FocusField`, `Dial` (memoised geometry; labels with a Night `paint-order: stroke` halo; `mini` for sleeves; the live trace has `pathLength=1` and `animate-trace motion-reduce:animate-none`, keyed by account so it draws again on a focus change), `MeterRow` (the safety cell gets the `safety-mark` utility, an inset Ember edge from `../index.css`), `Sleeves` (every account but the focused one; a click focuses it), `JournalTape` (ends with the Full journal link to `/live/journal`; shown even with nothing armed or the status failing, since it reads the journal on its own), `JournalRows`, `useJournal` (one `GET /api/live/journal?day=` for every account, disarmed ones included; `skip=bar&skip=peak&skip=day_start` for the tape, `skip=bar` for the full journal until its "Show bars" toggle; a failed read shows no rows)). `strip.tsx` (`SystemStrip`: New York clock, LSE feed (while it is down, its retry and reason in Red) and NinjaTrader as Label over Value, the large code strip with the alarm name in Red; `KillSwitch`: "Kill all" (red edge) opening the AlertDialog with the counts, or a line that they are unknown when the status failed; `CodeStrip`, `useNow`). `fixtures.ts`: status and journal fixtures (`desk()`, `deskJournal()`, `equity()`) for the Vitest and Playwright tests, never imported by the app.
- The kill switch never depends on the status: it shows and works while the status is loading or failing. A failed read (500, 401, …) is never shown as current: the page passes `undefined`, so the strip says "Status unavailable" and the dialog that the counts are unknown. `ok: false` from the kill is an alarm that says accounts may already be disarmed or halted; a request error says the kill may not have run. Either way the page re-reads the status.
- The focus field and sleeves show only a fresh status: while a read fails they are not drawn at all ("Accounts unavailable until the status reads again"); the tape and the full journal show only a fresh journal read. Resolve shows on the focus and on sleeves for `reconcile` and `drift` pauses; every answer, and any request error, re-reads the status (invalidates `['live']`, which also re-reads the journal). While a pick holds the focus, "Follow most severe" hands it back.
- Not here: the page shells and queries (`../routes/live.tsx`, `../routes/live-journal.tsx`); arming and disarming (Strategies page); live trading itself (`src/fenrir/live/`).
- Tests: `model.test.ts`, `dial.test.ts`, `journal.test.ts`, `actions.test.ts` (Vitest); the page render is `../routes/live.test.tsx` (focus, trace classes, safety mark, Full journal link); the dialog and kill flows, including a failing status, are `web/e2e/kill-switch.spec.ts`; focus (a new alarm takes it), the dial redraw on focus change only, sleeves, meters, reduced motion, the tape, the full journal with its strip and Kill all, Resolve (200, 404, 500, disabled across a page change) and failed reads are `web/e2e/live.spec.ts` (API mocked with `page.route`).
