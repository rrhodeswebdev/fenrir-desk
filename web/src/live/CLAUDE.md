# web/src/live/

The parts of the Live page (`../routes/live.tsx`) over `GET /api/live/status` and `POST /api/live/kill` (`docs/contracts.md` "Code strips" and the live API lines beside it; DESIGN.md "Top Strip", "Code Strip", "Kill-Switch Confirmation").

- Entry points: `model.ts` (pure: `LiveStatus`/`AccountStatus`/`KillResult` types; `systemStrip` (Feed · Broker · Drift per the contract's code-strip rules: Feed alarm when LSE is down, caution for any `stale` pause; Broker alarm when NinjaTrader is down, idle while every account is a dry run, so also with none armed; Drift caution when `drift.ok` is false), `stripLabel` (the `aria-label`), `need` (an account's Book or Risk cell, or "Halt" for a halt no cell names; stale and drift pauses are the system strip's, never an account's), `mostSevere` (alarm before caution, then live, sim, dry run, then Book, Risk, Halt; ties keep the server's order), `alarmName` (the most severe account in alarm as `<cell> · <account>`; a caution is not named), `nyTime`, `age`, `killCounts`, `killText`). `strip.tsx` (`SystemStrip`: New York clock, LSE feed and NinjaTrader as Label over Value, the large code strip with the alarm name in Red; `KillSwitch`: "Kill all" (red edge) opening the AlertDialog with the counts, or a line that they are unknown when the status failed; `CodeStrip`, `useNow`). `fixtures.ts`: status fixtures for the Vitest and Playwright tests, never imported by the app.
- The kill switch never depends on the status: it shows and works while the status is loading or failing. `ok: false` from the kill is an alarm that says accounts may already be disarmed or halted; a request error says the kill may not have run. Either way the page re-reads the status.
- Not here: the page shell and query (`../routes/live.tsx`); the focus field, dial, meters, sleeves, Resolve and journal tape (#98); live trading itself (`src/fenrir/live/`).
- Tests: `model.test.ts` (Vitest, pure); the page render is `../routes/live.test.tsx`; the dialog and kill flows, including a failing status, are `web/e2e/kill-switch.spec.ts` (API mocked with `page.route`).
