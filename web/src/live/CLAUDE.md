# web/src/live/

The parts of the Live page (`../routes/live.tsx`) and its full journal (`../routes/live-journal.tsx`) over `GET /api/live/status`, `GET /api/live/journal`, `POST /api/live/kill` and `POST /api/live/resolve` (`docs/contracts.md` "Code strips" and the live API lines beside it; DESIGN.md "Top Strip", "Code Strip", "Kill-Switch Confirmation", "Catalog-Number Headline", "Session Dial", "Segmented Meter", "Sleeve", "Journal Tape", "Motion").

- Entry points: `model.ts` (pure: `LiveStatus`/`AccountStatus`/`KillResult` types; `systemStrip` (Feed · Broker · Drift per the contract's code-strip rules: Feed idle with nothing armed, else alarm when LSE is down, caution for any `stale` pause; Broker alarm when NinjaTrader is down, idle while every account is a dry run, so also with none armed; Drift caution when `drift.ok` is false), `stripLabel` (the `aria-label`), `need` (an account's Book or Risk cell, or "Halt" for a halt no cell names; stale and drift pauses are the system strip's, never an account's), `mostSevere` (alarm before caution, then live, sim, dry run, then Book, Risk, Halt; ties keep the server's order), `alarmName` (the most severe account in alarm as `<cell> · <account>`; a caution is not named), `nyTime`, `age`, `killCounts` (dry-run positions counted apart as paper), `killText`, `killErrorText`, `KILL_TIMEOUT_MS` (90 s); for #98: `accountStrip` (Risk · Book), `focusOf` (the user's pick while armed, else `mostSevere`, else live, sim, dry run), `canResolve` (`reconcile` or `drift` only), `alertTone`, `usd` (cents only when there are any; `signed` for P&L), `price`, `positionText`, `bracketText`, `meters` (drawdown left, daily loss left or "No limit", contracts in minis, target in evaluation; the safety fraction is where the session's `safety` equity level falls on that meter), `segments` (20 cells: Steel, Ember past the safety fraction, Red at 100% of a limit only), `meterLabel`, `resolveText` (404 = "not running", never "not armed"; 500 = the pause may already be lifted)). `dial.ts` (pure geometry in a 600 viewBox: angle 18:00 → 17:00 New York, radius floor → target (funded: as far above the start as the floor is below); rings with at-risk flags and labels above their rings pushed a line apart; overnight/live split at 09:30; flat-by spoke at 16:40, the runner's cap, since the status has no `flat_by`; `dialLabel`). `journal.ts` (pure: `JournalRow`, `tradingDay` (New York date six hours on, as `core.sessions.trading_day`), `merge` newest first, `tape` (latest 12, no `bar` or `peak` rows), `journalLine` (`halt` of payload kind `reconcile` → MISMATCH Red; other halts and alerts Ember; `resolve` → RESOLVE and rejections → REJECT in Slate), `journalPollMs` (5 s)). `parts.tsx` (`FocusField`, `Dial` (`mini` for sleeves; the live trace has `pathLength=1` and `animate-trace motion-reduce:animate-none`, keyed by account so it draws again on a focus change), `MeterRow` (the safety cell gets the `safety-mark` utility, an inset Ember edge from `../index.css`), `Sleeves` (every account but the focused one; a click focuses it), `JournalTape` (ends with the Full journal link to `/live/journal`), `JournalRows`, `useJournal` (one `['live', 'journal', account, day]` query per armed account)). `strip.tsx` (`SystemStrip`: New York clock, LSE feed and NinjaTrader as Label over Value, the large code strip with the alarm name in Red; `KillSwitch`: "Kill all" (red edge) opening the AlertDialog with the counts, or a line that they are unknown when the status failed; `CodeStrip`, `useNow`). `fixtures.ts`: status and journal fixtures (`desk()`, `deskJournal()`, `equity()`) for the Vitest and Playwright tests, never imported by the app.
- The kill switch never depends on the status: it shows and works while the status is loading or failing. A failed read (500, 401, …) is never shown as current: the page passes `undefined`, so the strip says "Status unavailable" and the dialog that the counts are unknown. `ok: false` from the kill is an alarm that says accounts may already be disarmed or halted; a request error says the kill may not have run. Either way the page re-reads the status.
- The focus field, sleeves and tape show only a fresh status: while a read fails they are not drawn at all ("Accounts unavailable until the status reads again"). Resolve shows on the focus and on sleeves for `reconcile` and `drift` pauses; every answer, and any request error, re-reads the status (invalidates `['live']`, which also re-reads the journal). The journal API takes one account per call, so the tape and the full journal cover the accounts the status lists (an account disarmed earlier today is not shown).
- Not here: the page shells and queries (`../routes/live.tsx`, `../routes/live-journal.tsx`); arming and disarming (Strategies page); live trading itself (`src/fenrir/live/`).
- Tests: `model.test.ts`, `dial.test.ts`, `journal.test.ts` (Vitest, pure); the page render is `../routes/live.test.tsx` (focus, trace classes, safety mark, Full journal link); the dialog and kill flows, including a failing status, are `web/e2e/kill-switch.spec.ts`; focus, sleeves, meters, reduced motion, the tape, the full journal and Resolve (200, 404, 500) are `web/e2e/live.spec.ts` (API mocked with `page.route`).
